# 2026.260.1158 — fix(candy): move the skill to the candy-cli family

## Summary

Moves the `candy` skill's `family:` to `candy-cli`.

**Why.** The marketplace repo's own source tree lives at `candy/`
(`candy/charly-marketplace/charly.yml` = the refs list). A family named `candy`
is emitted to `candy/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` and `candy/.claude-plugin/`, i.e.
into that source tree — proven when the corpus regeneration wrote generated
manifests into `candy/.claude-plugin/`. opencharly/pod-charly-hooks#7 renames the
family to `candy-cli`; this moves the declaration with it.
